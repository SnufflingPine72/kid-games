Pollen Quilt

Perch · bees here drop pollen into the dashed rows
Hive lanes · tap the front bee to send it out

Each bee carries a different amount of pollen, shown by its number, and a big load holds its perch until every drop is used. The quilt fills from the bottom, two rows at a time. A perched bee only drops pollen on a dashed square of its own color. When every perch is full and nobody can drop anything, the hive jams. Shoo mode lets you tap a perched bee to send it to the back of a lane.
